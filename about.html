About The Project

This aggregator provides real-time financial crime, sanctions, and regulatory intelligence gathered directly from key investigative newsrooms and government bodies.

Instead of relying on automated noise, this platform curates high-value updates from dedicated investigative outlets and official registers, including:

  • OCCRP & ICIJ: Global investigative reporting on money laundering and transnational corruption.
  • FinCEN (Federal Register): Official U.S. treasury advisories, rulemakings, and GTOs.
  • Bellingcat: Open-source intelligence and asset tracking.
  • Transparency International & LSM: European regional integrity and compliance news.

UPDATE FREQUENCY Feeds refresh every 15 minutes to display verified intelligence from the past 14 days.
