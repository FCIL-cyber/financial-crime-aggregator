About the project

Financial Crime Intelligence aggregates selected sources of real-time intelligence associated with financial crime and its prevention by normalizing fragmented RSS and Atom feeds into a single unified stream.

Designed for like minded professionals who want to up to date with current affairs but do not want to be flooded with information

This is an open-source project. We do not and have no intention of monetizing it. If you have an suggestion or just want to chat, hit us up on LinkedIn or Substack.

⚡ Feeds automatically update every 30 minutes
