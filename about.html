About the project

Financial Crime Intelligence aggregates real-time data on financial crime and its prevention, normalizing fragmented RSS and Atom feeds into a single unified stream.

Built for like-minded professionals who want to stay informed without getting flooded with noise.

This is a non-commercial, open-source project available on GitHub. If you have suggestions or want to connect, reach out to us on LinkedIn or Substack.
